03 / About

Artistic intent.
Technical thinking.

PROFILE & SKILLS

I work with procedural tools and visual effects to help bring a director's vision to the screen.

Based in Paris, I'm a visual effects artist focused on FX and Houdini-based workflows. My background in compositing gave me an appreciation for the relationship between individual elements and the final image.

I developed my FX practice through hands-on Houdini work, technical exploration and production experience, including feature film work at DNEG and Mac Guff.

I enjoy the balance between artistic problem-solving and technical experimentation: understanding the brief, building a workable setup, and refining the result with the team.
